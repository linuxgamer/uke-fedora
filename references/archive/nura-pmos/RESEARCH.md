# Historical Nura/postmarketOS Research

This was the initial hardware, stock-firmware, and donor-project survey for a
Nura/postmarketOS port. Its durable factual findings were moved into the active
stock analysis documents:

- `docs/stock-dtb.md` for boot images, partitions, DTB/DTBO, and board wiring.
- `docs/stock-super.md` for firmware and stock module inventory.
- `docs/hardware-notes.md` for the concise hardware summary.

Its Nura/pmbootstrap workflow assumptions are obsolete.
